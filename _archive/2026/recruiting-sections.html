ANC 8E05 has nobody on the ballot. You can change that.

Our seat on Advisory Neighborhood Commission 8E is open. On November 3, whoever gets the most write-in votes wins it, as long as they're eligible and file one form with the Board of Elections. This page explains how to run, who's running, and how to vote for them.

Run as a write-in

You don't need petitions or a fee. You need to be eligible, file one notarized form, and get your neighbors to correctly write in your name on the ballot.

Who can run

  • A registered DC voter...
  • ...who lives in SMD 8E05... (The Board of Elections' form ties residency to 60 days before the petition deadline; call the Board at (202) 727-2525 to confirm you qualify.)
  • ...and who holds no other elected office.

How to run

  1. Answer the unofficial candidate questionnaire here by October 24. This is not required to run, but everyone who completes it will be listed on this page with their answers as a reference for voters.
  2. Download and fill out the affirmation form, but don't sign it yet. Get the Affirmation of Write-in Candidacy from the Board of Elections. For the office, write "Advisory Neighborhood Commissioner, SMD 8E05."
  3. Sign the form in front of a notary or a Board of Elections official. The Board's office is at 1015 Half Street SE, Suite 750, open Monday to Friday, 8:15 a.m. to 4:45 p.m. Many banks also offer free notary service to customers.
  4. File it with the Board of Elections. The legal deadline is 5 p.m. on Tuesday, November 10. File ASAP, before Election Day if you can, so your name appears on the Board's public list of write-in candidates.
  5. Tell your neighbors exactly how to write your name. Use the same spelling everywhere.

How candidates are listed

  • Anyone who completes the questionnaire and its eligibility statements will be listed as a candidate.
  • Responses are published as submitted.
  • Candidates appear in alphabetical order by last name.
  • The last day to be added is October 24, ten days before the election.
  • "Affirmation filed" means I have confirmed that the candidate has filed. Otherwise the listing says "Not yet confirmed." You can find the BOE's official List of General Election Write-in Candidates here.
